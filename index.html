About

From the Air Force to ownership

I grew up in southwest Louisiana and joined the Air Force at 19. Twenty years in uniform taught me how to lead, how to make decisions without perfect information, and how to take responsibility when it actually matters.

The Air Force also brought me to Nebraska, where I met my wife. We're raising our three daughters here in Omaha. Everything I build, I build with them in mind.

After I retired, I spent about five years working for the government as a contractor while I built on the side. I got my start in real estate, buying and holding rental property. That's what opened my eyes to ownership.

From real estate I made my way into business. Instead of starting one from scratch and hoping it worked, I bought one that already did. A real company, with real customers and a real team.

Today I run my real estate and my business full time. Everything I build, I build to last. For my wife, our three daughters, and the people around us.

Ryan Miller · Founder, AVARA Investments

The Approach

A business is the cash engine. Real estate is the vault.

The strategy is simple. Buy a business that already makes money. Keep what it produces through a high savings rate. Then park that profit in real estate, where it compounds, shelters income from taxes, and keeps working long after the business does. One makes the money. The other keeps it, grows it, and passes it on.

01Acquire

Buy established, cash-flowing businesses. Skip the highest-failure part of the journey.

02Reinvest

Move profit into buy-and-hold, value-add real estate. Long-term wealth, near-term tax benefit, real cash flow.

03Build

Run lean with people, systems, and AI. Build something durable, paid for, and worth passing down.
