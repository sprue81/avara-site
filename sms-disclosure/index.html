SMS Notification Program

This page describes the SMS notification system operated by AVARA Holdings. It exists for compliance verification with U.S. wireless carriers and Twilio's A2P 10DLC registration program.

Program Description

AVARA Holdings operates a private, single-user internal business notification system. The sole recipient is the account owner, Ryan Miller, who is also the founder and operator of AVARA Holdings. No other end users receive messages. No marketing, promotional, or third-party messages are ever sent through this number.

Messages are sent on demand or on a fixed schedule, and contain operational data the owner has explicitly configured the system to deliver to himself: accounts receivable summaries, team utilization reports, scheduled reminders, and ad hoc system status updates.

Sample Messages

AR Report (as of 2026-04-19): Total Outstanding $179,631.62. Top balance: BHI Companies $47,288.50. 60+ days overdue: 3 invoices.
Inbox digest 7am CT: 4 emails awaiting decision. JR Woltemath replied on tax question. Earl SOD report logged.
Crew utilization Tue: 67% real, 6 techs in field, $4,820 billable revenue. No flags.

How Opt-In Occurred

The account owner is the only recipient. Opt-in occurred when the owner provisioned this Twilio number on his own Twilio account, registered his personal mobile phone number as the sole destination address inside the system's configuration files, and enabled the notification flag himself. There is no public sign-up form because there are no other recipients.

Express written consent is on file in the form of the system's environment configuration, which is owned, edited, and maintained exclusively by Ryan Miller.

Message Frequency

Low volume. Approximately 10 to 60 messages per month, varying with operational events. Most days zero messages are sent.

Opt-Out and Help

  • Reply STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, or REVOKE to opt out of all future messages.
  • Reply HELP or INFO to receive program information.
  • Standard message and data rates may apply, charged by your wireless carrier.

Privacy

No phone numbers, message content, or personal data are sold, shared, or transferred to any third party for any purpose. The only parties with access to message content are AVARA Holdings (the sender) and the recipient's wireless carrier (for delivery).

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
